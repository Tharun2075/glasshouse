How a gift travels

Five stops, all of them on the record.

  1. 01 · Give

    A donor gives through PayPal

    The gift is tied to an appeal, such as Winter Food Parcels, and appears on the public ledger straight away.

  2. 02 · Receipt

    A volunteer uploads the receipt

    Money only leaves with proof. Each spend starts as a photo of a real receipt, attached to its row.

  3. 03 · AI check

    Claude reads it and flags risk

    Totals, supplier and history are checked. A spend far above the usual, or a likely duplicate, is held in amber.

  4. 04 · Approve

    A trustee makes the call

    A person, not the model, approves or rejects. The reason is written next to the row for anyone to read.

  5. 05 · Payout

    PayPal pays the supplier

    PayPal Payouts sends the money and the batch id is stamped on the row, so the trail ends at a real transfer.
